## 1. Getting Started

Your journey begins by creating an account and setting up your preferences so PeraWave can tailor content specifically for you.

### Registration Process
To join the PeraWave community:
1. Navigate to the **Welcome** page and click on the **Register** button.
2. **Email Verification:** Enter a valid email address. This will be used for account recovery and important notifications.
3. **Account Details:** Fill in your desired Username, a secure Password, and any other requested profile details. Choose a unique username that represents you in the community!
4. **Select Interests:** This is a crucial step! By selecting topics you are interested in (e.g., Technology, Sports, Arts, Academics), PeraWave will automatically customize your Home feed to show discussions and events most relevant to you.
5. Once completed, you will be automatically redirected to the login page.

### Logging In
1. Go to the **Login** page.
2. Enter your registered email address and your password.
3. Click **Login** to access your dashboard.

### Recovering Your Account
If you ever forget your password:
1. Click on the **Forgot Password** link on the Login page.
2. Enter the email address associated with your account.
3. Check your inbox for a secure reset link. Click it to create a new, secure password and regain access to your account.

## 2. User Profile & Dashboard

Your personal space on PeraWave allows you to track your activity and customize how others see you.

### Your Personal Dashboard
The **User Dashboard** acts as your personal command center. Whenever you log in, you can use the dashboard to:
- **Track Activity:** View a history of your recent posts, comments, and interactions.
- **Quick Access:** Seamlessly jump back into forums you frequently visit or events you are attending.
- **Notifications & Updates:** Keep an eye on replies to your posts or updates to events you are interested in.

### Customizing Your Profile
Your **User Profile** is how the PeraWave community sees you.
- **Profile Details:** Update your display name, add a short biography to introduce yourself, and upload a profile picture.
- **Content History:** Your profile acts as a portfolio of your contributions. Others can view the posts you've authored and the public events you are hosting.
- **Managing Interests:** As your hobbies change, you can revisit the **Interests** section to update your preferences. This ensures your Home feed remains fresh and relevant.

## 3. Forums & Posts

The Forums are the heartbeat of PeraWave. It’s where discussions happen, questions get answered, and ideas are shared.

### The Home Feed vs. My Forums
- **Home Feed:** This page aggregates top posts and trending discussions specifically tailored to the interests you selected during registration. It’s the best place to discover new content.
- **My Forums:** This tab lists the specific community sub-forums you have actively joined. If you want to dive deep into a specific topic without distractions, navigate here.

### Creating a New Post
Got something to share? Starting a discussion is easy:
1. Navigate to your desired forum or click the **Create Post** button on your feed.
2. **Title:** Give your post a clear, catchy title so users know exactly what it's about.
3. **Content:** Write your thoughts in the main text area. You can structure your post, ask questions, or share updates.
4. **Attachments:** Depending on the forum's rules, you may be able to attach images or external links to enrich your post.
5. Click **Submit** to publish. Your post will instantly be visible to the community.

### Interacting with the Community
Engaging with others is highly encouraged!
- **Viewing Details:** Click on any post card to open the **Post Detail** view, where you can read the full content and see all replies.
- **Comments:** Leave a comment to share your opinion, answer a question, or encourage the author.
- **Reactions:** Use likes or upvotes to show appreciation for high-quality posts. This helps the best content rise to the top of the feed!

## 4. Events

Whether it's a study group, a tech meetup, or a social gathering, PeraWave makes it easy to organize and discover events.

### Discovering Events
- Navigate to the **Events** tab to browse a calendar and list of upcoming community gatherings.
- Use filters to find events happening this week, or search for events matching your specific interests.
- Click on any event to view the **Event Detail** page. Here you will find the exact date, time, location (or virtual meeting link), and a detailed description provided by the host.

### Hosting Your Own Event
Want to bring people together? 
1. Click the **Create Event** button.
2. Provide a descriptive title and a compelling description.
3. Set the **Date, Time, and Location**. Be as specific as possible so attendees know exactly where to go.
4. Categorize your event so it reaches the right audience.
5. Click **Publish**. Users can now discover your event, RSVP, and start discussing it in the event's comment section.

## 5. Wiki Articles

The PeraWave Wiki is a collaborative, ever-growing knowledge base written by the community, for the community. It's the perfect place to find tutorials, academic guides, and detailed references.

### Browsing for Knowledge
- Go to the **Wiki List** to see the directory of available articles, sorted by category and popularity.
- Click on an article to open the **Wiki Article Detail** page. 
- Use the search function to quickly find specific guides or topics you need help with.

### Contributing to the Wiki
If you have expertise on a topic, share it with the community!
1. Click **Create Wiki Article**.
2. **Title & Category:** Choose a concise title and place the article in the correct category so others can easily find it.
3. **Drafting the Content:** Use clear headings, bullet points, and formatting to make your article easy to read. A good wiki article is factual, well-organized, and comprehensive.
4. **Publishing:** Once submitted, your article becomes part of the permanent PeraWave knowledge base, helping countless other users in the future!
